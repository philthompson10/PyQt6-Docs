This signal is emitted when the :sip:ref:`~PyQt6.QtMultimedia.QWindowCapture.error` or :sip:ref:`~PyQt6.QtMultimedia.QWindowCapture.errorString` properties are changed.

This signal is not emitted whenever multiple identical errors are raised. To track such errors, use the signal :sip:ref:`~PyQt6.QtMultimedia.QWindowCapture.errorOccurred`.
