Storage buffer. This allows the :sip:ref:`~PyQt6.QtGui.QRhiBuffer` to be used in combination with :sip:ref:`~PyQt6.QtGui.QRhiShaderResourceBinding.Type.BufferLoad`, :sip:ref:`~PyQt6.QtGui.QRhiShaderResourceBinding.Type.BufferStore`, or :sip:ref:`~PyQt6.QtGui.QRhiShaderResourceBinding.Type.BufferLoadStore`.
