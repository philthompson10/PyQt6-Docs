Indicates that the ``gl_BaseInstance``, ``gl_BaseVertex`` and ``gl_DrawID`` built-in variables are available in shaders. In practice this can be expected to be supported on Vulkan 1.1+ and with desktop OpenGL 4.6 or ``GL_ARB_shader_draw_parameters``.
