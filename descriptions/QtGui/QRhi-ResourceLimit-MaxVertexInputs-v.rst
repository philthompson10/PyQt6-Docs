The number of input attributes to the vertex shader. The location in a :sip:ref:`~PyQt6.QtGui.QRhiVertexInputAttribute` must be in range ``[0, MaxVertexInputs-1]``. The value may be as low as 8 with OpenGL ES 2.0. Elsewhere, typical values are 16, 31, or 32.
